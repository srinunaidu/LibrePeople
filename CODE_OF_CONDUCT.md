# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** as its
proposed code of conduct.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 is the authoritative
reference and is available at:

- <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>

## Our Standards

Per Contributor Covenant 2.1, contributors pledge to make participation a
harassment-free experience for everyone, including but not limited to
behaviour such as:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy towards other community members.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, trolling, insulting or
derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, publishing others'
private information without permission, and any other conduct deemed
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards
of acceptable behaviour and will take appropriate and fair corrective
action in response to any behaviour they deem inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (issues, pull
requests, discussions, events) and when an individual is officially
representing the community in public spaces.
